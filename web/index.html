ONE RECORD, TWO SATELLITES

MODIS and VIIRS, on one scale.

Each cell counts fire cell-days: 0.1° grid cells with at least one detection that day. VIIRS sees more small fires than MODIS, so MODIS is scaled to match where the two overlap. Click a month to open it above.
