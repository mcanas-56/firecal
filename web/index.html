About this data and the season estimate ＋

Each cell counts harmonized fire cell-days: 0.1° grid cells with at least one detection on a day. The dashed line averages each calendar month across complete years, including the selected year when complete. Shading marks the estimated typical fire season. These are historical observations, not forecasts.
